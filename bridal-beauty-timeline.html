Planning your wedding is exciting, and your beauty routine deserves just as much attention as your dress. Here's a simple timeline to help you look radiant on your big day.

6 Months Before
  • Schedule beauty consultations.
  • Decide on your hairstyle.
  • Begin a healthy skincare routine.
  • Book your bridal beauty team.
3 Months Before
  • Hair coloring and treatments.
  • Eyebrow shaping.
  • Lash consultation.
  • Hair extension consultation.
1 Month Before
  • Bridal hair and makeup trial.
  • Final skincare treatment.
  • Nail inspiration selection.
1 Week Before
  • Fresh manicure and pedicure.
  • Eyelash extensions.
  • Hair conditioning treatment.
