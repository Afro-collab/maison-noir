Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking. Still have a question? Message us on WhatsApp any time.

What services does Rich Beauty Bar offer?

We offer Microblading, Ombre Powder Brows, Nail Services, Haircut & Styling, Braiding, Hair Color, Hair Extensions, Professional Makeup, and Eyelash Extensions — plus dedicated Silver, Gold, and Platinum Bridal Packages for wedding days and special events.

How much do your services cost?

Pricing varies by service: Nail Services start at $35, Haircut & Styling from $45, Hair Color from $95, Professional Makeup from $95, Microblading from $450, Ombre Powder Brows from $500, Braiding from $120, Eyelash Extensions from $120, and Hair Extensions from $250. Bridal Packages range from $350 (Silver) to $1,200 (Platinum). Message us with your desired service for an exact quote.

How do I book an appointment?

Booking is done entirely through WhatsApp — tap any "Book Now" button on the site, or fill out the booking form in the Contact section below, and we'll confirm your appointment directly in the chat.

Where is Rich Beauty Bar located?

Our studio is located at 1110 Fidler Ln, Silver Spring, MD 20910. We proudly serve clients across Chicago, Silver Spring, Maryland, and Virginia.

What are your business hours?

We're open Tuesday through Saturday, 9am–7pm. Sunday and Monday appointments are available by request — just message us on WhatsApp to check availability.

Do you offer bridal hair and makeup packages?

Yes — our Silver ($350), Gold ($650), and Platinum ($1,200) Bridal Packages each include a consultation and day-of touch-ups. Gold adds a trial session and eyelash extensions, while Platinum includes hair extensions if needed, a bridal trial, ceremony touch-ups, and travel within Chicago, MD & VA.

Do you travel to wedding or event venues?

Travel within Chicago, Maryland, and Virginia is included with our Platinum Luxury Bridal Package. For other services, message us on WhatsApp to ask about mobile availability for your event.

Can I book makeup for a graduation, prom, or photoshoot?

Absolutely — our Professional Makeup service (from $95) is tailored for weddings, bridal parties, graduations, birthdays, photoshoots, prom, and corporate events.

Is Rich Beauty Bar Black-owned?

Yes — Rich Beauty Bar is proudly Black-owned, founded to give women across Chicago, Silver Spring, Maryland and Virginia a beauty experience that matches the significance of their moments, from wedding days to the everyday.

What if I need to reschedule or cancel my appointment?

Message us on WhatsApp as soon as you know you need to change your appointment, and we'll do our best to find a time that works for you.
